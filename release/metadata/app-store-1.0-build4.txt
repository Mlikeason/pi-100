• 本机保留最近 200 局成绩。
• 自动保存练习，关闭应用后也能继续。
• 为 iPhone Duo 设计：展开后数字区与键盘以中缝为界左右分屏，合上时上下排列。 [UPDATED]
• 更大的触感按键，实时节奏条显示当前用时所处的档位（仅供参考，不影响计分）。 [UPDATED: new design]
• 支持大字体、VoiceOver 和外接键盘。

==================== App 隐私 ====================
事实（来自代码和 zhuhong.me/pi100/privacy/）：没有开发者服务器，没有分析或广告 SDK，没有 IDFA，不访问位置、通讯录、相机或麦克风。进度和成绩只保存在本机（UserDefaults 加一个 JSON 文件）。只有在用户主动在“成绩单”里点“连接 Game Center”后，App 才会通过 GameKit 的 GKLeaderboard.submitScore 把一局完美挑战的用时（毫秒）提交给 Apple。代码不读取任何 Game Center 玩家 ID（gamePlayerID、teamPlayerID 都没有用）。
建议答案：“不收集数据”。理由：开发者不接收任何数据；成绩由 Apple 的 Game Center 服务处理。
较保守的写法（需要你决定）：“收集数据 → 用户内容 → 游戏内容”，与用户关联：否，用于追踪：否，用途：App 功能。
隐私清单（PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy）：tracking false；UserDefaults CA92.1；SystemBootTime 35F9.1。

备注（英文）：
Pi 100 is a free, ad-free native SwiftUI memory game for learning the first 100 decimal digits of pi. No account is required. Practice, Challenge, Review and local history work offline.

Game Center is optional, accessed through Results (成绩单) > Connect Game Center. The leaderboard me.zhuhong.pi100.perfect100 accepts only 100/100 unassisted, uninterrupted Challenge completions; lower elapsed time (milliseconds) wins. Pausing or leaving the app invalidates that round for the global board while preserving local progress. Practice and Review rounds never submit.

Test: tap Begin (开始) in Practice and enter digits on the keypad. Hint reveals the next digit but removes credit for that position. Results are saved automatically when a round completes; missed and assisted positions can be reviewed. The pace bar is display-only and does not affect scoring. Settings includes privacy, support and local-data deletion.

Layout: on iPhone Duo unfolded (or any wide window) the digit board is on the left half and the keypad on the right, split at the fold; on the folded cover display and on standard iPhones they are stacked. Game progress persists across layout changes. No special permissions, ads, purchases or tracking SDKs.
