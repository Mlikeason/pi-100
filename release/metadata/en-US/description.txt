One hundred digits. One small step at a time.

Pi 100 is a focused, native way to memorize the first 100 decimal digits of pi. Practice at your own pace, revisit the digits you missed, then put your memory to the test.

• Practice with optional hints; assisted digits are tracked separately.
• Challenge yourself without hints and compare perfect, uninterrupted runs on Game Center.
• Review the exact positions you missed.
• Keep your latest 200 results on your device.
• Continue where you left off after closing the app.
• Unfold iPhone Duo to see more digits, with the familiar digit board above the keypad.
• Support for larger text, VoiceOver, and hardware keyboard input.

Free. No ads. No subscriptions. Practice works offline; Game Center needs an internet connection and an Apple account.
