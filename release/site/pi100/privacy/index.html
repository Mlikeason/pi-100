隐私政策

π 100 由 Zhu Hong 开发。本政策说明 iOS 应用的数据使用方式。

本机数据

练习进度、答题记录、最近 200 局成绩和偏好保存在设备上。应用不向开发者的服务器上传这些数据，也不包含广告或第三方分析 SDK。系统备份可能包含应用数据，取决于你的设备备份设置。

可选 Game Center

你主动连接 Game Center 后，符合规则的完美挑战成绩会提交给 Apple，用于排行榜。Apple 处理 Game Center 账户、玩家昵称和成绩展示；请参阅 Apple 的 Game Center 隐私说明。离线成绩可在后续连接时重试。开发者不收集你的 Apple 账户密码或邮箱。

删除与控制

可在应用“设置”中清除本机进度和成绩。该操作不会删除已提交给 Game Center 的排行榜成绩。你可以在系统设置中管理 Game Center，并联系支持咨询数据问题。

网站与支持

访问支持网站时，托管服务可能处理正常的请求日志（例如 IP 地址和浏览器信息）。发送支持邮件时，你提供的邮箱和邮件内容仅用于回复和处理问题。无需提供密码或验证码。

Privacy policy — English

Pi 100 stores progress, answers, preferences, and the latest 200 rounds on your device. The app does not upload these to a developer-operated server and includes no ads or third-party analytics. Device backups may include app data according to your backup settings.

When you choose to connect Game Center, eligible perfect challenge times are sent to Apple for leaderboard functionality. Apple manages your Game Center identity, nickname, and displayed scores. Pending offline scores can be retried after connection. We do not collect your Apple account password or email.

Settings lets you delete local progress and results. This does not remove scores already submitted to Game Center. Manage Game Center through system settings. Website hosting may process standard request logs. Support emails are used to respond to your request. Contact Zhu Hong at zivzhu@gmail.com.
